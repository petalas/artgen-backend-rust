# Source-coupled mutation workgroup filters

The original mutation dispatched four groups of 64 offspring lanes. These four
independent candidates keep all 256 logical offspring, their IDs, RNG streams,
mutation branches and parameters. They change physical grouping and cooperate
only on the parent copy before the original leader body. The original shader
remains the production default until full confirmation passes.

The frozen screen used two alternating pairs per project, eight measured batches,
two matching warmups and seed 7. All 48 timing trials and 15 separate overflow
replays passed seven-field canonical replay, raw framebuffer-byte L1, fitness,
complete submitted/completed work, device and timestamp checks. Timing work is
6,291,456 evaluations; diagnostic work is 1,966,080 and has no throughput score.
The three reference qualification runs contribute another 393,216 evaluations.
Every slow pair remains included. These two-pair intervals are exploratory.

Only g1 advanced, selected by the highest passing aggregate point estimate. Its
conditional 95% interval is [1.1032567785180345, 1.1239483759324873]. The fixed
confirmation contract is `mutation-group-confirmation/spec.json`: five pairs per
case at 64 batches, then the same schedule on distinct mature reserved states
with ff/mona/darwin seeds 11/13/17. The unchanged policy applies, with no runner-up
promotion, sample extensions or exclusions. A passing short filter is no adoption.

The source-derived logical width is checked before pipeline installation and
retained in the exact-source cache key. Both engine dispatch sites use
`[active, ceil(lambda / width), 1]`; construction derives the bundled width too.
Actual width is recorded outside GPU identity and MutationParams. Marker-free
historical sources retain width 64. Invalid, duplicated or commented declarations
fail closed. The selected source is SHA-256
`3a36417746ab19b0c800c718e8c87795c17d9bff78114fd65bc8369b016578d8`,
against historical `e13308a7cc59071043a3f5750a7f25214933ae4645cd016875f2dfa12c3dfe73`.

A deliberately corrupted grouped g1 shader changed the three final RNG writes.
The actual installer rejected final canonical replay, reported six errors and
withheld the candidate rate. Its valid reference work remains retained in
`mutation-group-negative-gate/`; this is not a timing sample.

The selected source then passed the actual mutation checker: all 368 fixtures,
736 submitted/completed dispatches and zero failed results. Its semantic checks
cover every integer lambda 1–64, single/multiple mutation, adaptive mode,
empty-parent crossover, polygon-count boundaries through 1000, source-isolated
retained histories and unchanged unproduced slots. Inactive padding is reported
separately and is not silently promoted to active genotype. No evolution score
is produced by that direct checker. The proof is candidate-specific.

Independent static review confirmed all 16 original helpers byte-for-byte, and
the original leader body after removing its serial copy. Every parent-prefix slot
has one loader and one copy writer; both unconditional barriers precede returns.
The storage barrier separates cooperative writes from leader mutation. Added
copy/load loops take at most 16 iterations per lane. Empty-parent crossover keeps
the default-zeroed slot-zero fallback; logical child zero retains its adaptive
exception. Tournament selection keeps the complete 1024-parent binding, including
inactive donors. No probability, precision, objective or layer order changes.

The long mutation entry point remains a documented Power of Ten deviation: its
RNG-consuming branch sequence is kept together and byte-pinned rather than
refactored during this execution experiment. Direct fixture coverage and exact
trajectory gates compensate; they do not establish hardware occupancy. Duplicated
parent loads and shared initialization remain real work, so more groups alone
were only a hypothesis before measurement.

Evidence under `.cache/evolution-overnight-20261024/`: `mutation-split/` contains
the generator, hashes and original diffs; `mutation-group-screen-spec.json` is the
pre-data schedule; `mutation-group-screen-01/output/` retains raw rows and complete
rates/ranges; `mutation-group-confirmation/numerical/` retains direct GPU proof.
Immutable `build-v8/` records actual Cargo executable paths, release profile,
sources and binary hashes. Patches 28–31 apply independently to the pinned e133
fixture. The kept binner is 598b32 and the fixed raster is AE1 (report 11).

Background isolation remains unverified on the recorded RTX 5090 Vulkan/Dozen
backend. Sampled mutation intervals can support attribution but are not whole-run
shares or hardware-utilization measurements. Full training, reserved and endpoint
audits remain the adoption gates.
